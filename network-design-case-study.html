01

Overview & my role

I developed an academic design proposal for the fictional PhotoHut business as part of my IT studies at Purdue University Global. This completed planning project was not deployed to a production environment.

02

The technical challenge

Plan a network upgrade that brings logical architecture, physical infrastructure, equipment selection, security, budgeting, and user training into one documented proposal.

03

Architecture & technologies

The proposal covers topology, IP addressing, subnetting, routing and switching considerations, segmentation, and physical layout. The original capstone contains the design documentation.

Business requirements↓Logical & physical network design↓Security, equipment & delivery plan

Conceptual overview of the proposal, not a production network diagram.

04

Implementation planning

Documented equipment, budget considerations, implementation planning, and training materials. The deliverable is a proposal rather than a record of installed systems.

05

Security & testing

Network segmentation and security policies form part of the design. No live deployment tests or production validation results are claimed.

06

Results & lessons

The completed proposal demonstrates infrastructure planning and technical documentation. Considering equipment, security, budgets, and training together highlights how technical choices depend on operational needs.
