01 / Project Overview

A modernization plan for office and studio.

PhotoHut was a fictional academic business modernization scenario for a growing photography company. The project combined office and studio network design, centralized server infrastructure, security planning, procurement research, scheduling, and simulation-based network work.

Academic design & simulation — not production deployment

The original report uses scenario language such as “implemented” and “installed.” Here, those activities are described as designed, proposed, modeled, planned, or simulated. No verified live installation or measured production outcome is claimed.

02 / Business Challenge

Move beyond a peer-to-peer environment.

The scenario started with an existing peer-to-peer network. Growth introduced a need for centralized services, additional workstations, office and studio connectivity, stronger security practices, and room for future expansion.

The design had to connect business needs with physical space, equipment compatibility, budget, security, and a practical transition plan.

03 / My Role

Academic project management and network design.

  4. Office workstation and studio equipment deployment planning.

13 / Lessons Learned

Architecture is only one part of delivery.

Start with business requirements

Office workflows, studio needs, and future growth shape the infrastructure design.

Design for the physical setting

Space, rack equipment, cabling, power, and environmental needs belong alongside the logical network.

Connect security and operations

Access, backups, privacy, training, and maintainability need an operating plan as well as technical controls.

Keep planning artifacts consistent

Budget versions, schedules, equipment comparisons, and presentations should be reconciled before treating a plan as ready for execution.
