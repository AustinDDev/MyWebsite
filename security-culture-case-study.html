Academic case study

Security is also
a people problem.

Completed cybersecurity research examining how organizational culture, leadership, and everyday behavior influence cyber risk.

In this studyOverview & my roleThe research challengeResearch approachSecurity considerationsTesting & evidenceResults & lessons
01

Overview & my role

As part of my Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity at Purdue University Global, I researched the human and organizational dimensions of information security.

02

The research challenge

Explore how security awareness, employee behavior, leadership, policies, and accountability complement technical safeguards.

03

Research approach

The project examined organizational practices and cyber risk, focusing on practical security habits and communication. It was academic research, not a workplace intervention or deployed security product.

Leadership & policy↓Awareness & everyday behavior↓Organizational security practices

Conceptual research themes, not measured causal results.

04

Security considerations

Accountability, awareness, and organizational policy support technical controls. This summary contains no employer information or internal security material.

05

Testing & evidence

This was a research project; implementation tests and measured operational outcomes do not apply. The full research paper is not currently included in this repository.

06

Results & lessons

The completed research demonstrates risk analysis and security communication. Its central theme is the importance of considering people and organizational practices alongside technology.
