About Me

I'm a Junior Developer with a foundation in software engineering, network design, and cybersecurity. I'm passionate about creating modern, clean, and accessible user interfaces — experiences that not only work well but feel effortless.

With a background that blends technical infrastructure and security awareness, I bring a practical, systems-level understanding to front-end development and UI/UX work. I value logic, clarity, and purpose in everything I build.

When I'm not writing code, you'll find me spending time with family, perfecting my BBQ skills, diving into competitive gaming, or exploring how AI is transforming the modern workplace. I enjoy staying curious and continuously improving my craft.

Career

Before transitioning into front-end development and UX design, I worked in commercial real estate as a Project Manager — leading land acquisition, site planning, and multi-stakeholder collaboration efforts across high-impact developments. These experiences sharpened my communication, problem-solving, and systems-thinking skills, all of which I now bring to the world of digital product creation.

Today, I focus on crafting user-centered web applications and responsive interfaces. As I grow in my role as a developer, I continue building projects with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React, applying both technical and creative thinking to real-world solutions. My approach is rooted in clarity, accessibility, and continuous learning — always keeping the user at the center of every decision.
