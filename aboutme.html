01 / My story

The details matter.
So do the people.

I currently work in IT Desktop Support at Gaylor Electric, where I support enterprise endpoints, identity systems, and collaboration technology. I troubleshoot day-to-day issues, assist with deployments and onboarding, and work alongside other IT teams to keep people productive.

Before Gaylor, I worked in IT operations and service desk leadership at SelectQuote / SelectRx. I supported production technology, helped technicians work through difficult issues, coordinated escalations, and improved documentation. That experience reinforced the value of clear communication and dependable service delivery when technical issues affect daily operations.

My earlier work at EdgeRock Development connected vendor research, market analysis, and stakeholder communication with project decisions. On the athletic facility project, my involvement was concentrated in the early and later stages: investigating issues, researching options, presenting recommendations for approval, and coordinating assigned actions or handoffs.

I hold a completed Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity from Purdue University Global. That foundation helps me consider security alongside reliability and usability. Across these experiences, I have learned to connect technical details with clear communication, practical processes, and the business needs behind the work.
