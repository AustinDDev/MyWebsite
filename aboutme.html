About me

Technical depth.
People-first leadership.

I'm Austin Dalton, an IT operations professional with team leadership experience based in Westfield, Indiana. My background combines enterprise-scale IT operations, operational improvement, physical infrastructure support, cybersecurity education, and project management.

In a service desk lead and site lead role at SelectQuote / SelectRx, I helped lead a three-person technical team within an organization with more than 4,000 employees and an IT environment of approximately 1,780 Windows and 2,500 Apple devices. Those figures describe the organization's wider fleet, not devices I individually administered. I contributed to technical documentation, improved daily operational checklists and printer ink-ordering workflows, and supported complex technical escalations across enterprise systems.

Before entering enterprise IT, I spent approximately a decade managing commercial real estate development projects. Coordinating vendors, budgets, timelines, and stakeholders taught me to make decisions under pressure, communicate clearly, and balance operational needs with long-term outcomes.

I completed a Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity at Purdue University Global, following an Associate of Applied Science in Information Technology. Those degrees strengthened my understanding of network architecture, risk, access control, security culture, and the relationship between people, processes, and technology.

My hands-on experience also includes helping set up hardware in server rooms and running Ethernet cabling. I continue learning through programming, web development, PowerShell, Git/GitHub, and experimentation with automation. Coaching student athletes has also shaped how I mentor others, build accountability, and help teams work toward shared goals.
