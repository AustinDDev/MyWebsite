About me

Technical depth.
People-first leadership.

I'm Austin Dalton, an IT operations professional and technical leader based in Westfield, Indiana. My experience brings together enterprise IT support, cybersecurity education, technical project management, and mentoring.

As a IT Service Desk Lead and Site Lead at SelectQuote / SelectRx, I lead a team of three and help resolve complex enterprise technology challenges. My work includes advanced escalation coordination across Windows endpoints, Microsoft 365, identity systems, networking, and other infrastructure-related issues. I collaborate with internal teams and vendors, mentor colleagues, and improve documentation and support workflows.

Before entering enterprise IT, I spent approximately a decade managing commercial real estate development projects. Coordinating vendors, budgets, timelines, and stakeholders taught me to make decisions under pressure, communicate clearly, and balance operational needs with long-term outcomes.

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity from Purdue University Global in December 2025, after completing an Associate of Applied Science in Information Technology in 2023. Those degrees strengthened my understanding of network architecture, risk, access control, security culture, and the relationship between people, processes, and technology.

I continue learning through web development, PowerShell, Git/GitHub, and experimentation with automation. Coaching student athletes has also shaped how I mentor others, build accountability, and help teams work toward shared goals.
